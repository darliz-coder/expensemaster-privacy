
# ExpenseMaster Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

ExpenseMaster is a personal expense tracking application developed by Okwudiri Darlington.

## Information We Collect

ExpenseMaster stores your financial records, budgets, and settings locally on your device. We do not collect or sell your personal information.

## Permissions

The app may request:
- Storage access for exporting PDF reports and backups.
- Biometric authentication if you enable fingerprint unlock.

## Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with third parties.

## Data Security

Your information remains on your device unless you choose to export or back it up.

## Children's Privacy

ExpenseMaster is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added.
